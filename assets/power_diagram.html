Power Diagram Sandbox

- Pan: drag, or two-finger swipe on a trackpad
- Zoom: mouse wheel / trackpad pinch (touch: pinch)
- Reset view: double-click (touch: double-tap)
- Add point: click on empty space
- Move point: drag its center dot
- Adjust radius: drag the edge or the ■ handle
- Delete point: Shift + click on the circle
- Hover: highlights the cell under the cursor, its circle (green)
  and its RT neighbors' circles (dashed magenta)
With >100 points only the selected circle's edge is draggable;
dragging anywhere else pans.
